What If It Happens

A science channel on YouTube.

What if the Sun vanished for 24 hours? What if the Earth stopped spinning for one second? Each episode takes one impossible question and follows it minute by minute, using real physics, published research and real numbers.

About this app

"What If It Happens" is also the name of the small publishing tool the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and shorts to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner, only for the channel @whatifitreallyhappens, and it does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

Google account access is requested only to upload and manage videos on that channel. See the Privacy Policy for details.
